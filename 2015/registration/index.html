Registration

Attending the conference is free of charge, however advance registration is required.

If you are planning on attending the conference - please register now! Registration page is at https://appsecil2015.eventbrite.com/.

From there you will also be able to sign up at Sched.org, to view the conference schedule interactively, add sessions to your personal calendar, and other features.
