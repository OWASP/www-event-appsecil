➡️ 18:40 - Don’t Wait for Cyber Threats to Strike: How Proactive Threat Hunting Can Protect Your Network

Shani Kurtzberg - Threat Hunter @ Cato Networks

Ofir Blaicher - Threat Hunter @ Cato Networks

Threat hunting has become a critical component of modern cybersecurity, as adversaries can often go undetected for extended periods while inside a network. In this session, we will focus on the importance of proactive threat hunting to identify and eliminate potential or actual cybersecurity threats within an organization’s systems or networks. We will delve into the process of creating reliable and accurate security intelligence feeds using a combination of automated tools and manual analysis to identify indicators of compromise (IOCs). Additionally, we will explore the creation of industry-grade security intelligence feeds by leveraging multiple known and unknown sources tailored to an organization’s specific needs. Our discussion will also include how proactive threat hunting can reveal the tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) used by attackers, which can inform the development of robust security strategies.
