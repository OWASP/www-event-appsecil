## Get chapter updates

**The chapter has moved away from Meetup for announcements.** The mailing list is the place to hear about future events.

1. Email [israel-chapter+subscribe@owasp.org](mailto:israel-chapter+subscribe@owasp.org).
2. Follow the instructions in the confirmation email: click “Join this Group” or reply to complete your subscription.

You can also [join through Google Groups](https://groups.google.com/a/owasp.org/g/israel-chapter). Choose “Each email” to receive announcements.

## Get involved

- **Speak at a meetup:** share your research, experience, or something useful you have built. [Submit a talk](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeKvD3QipueLRAddpFiizoYAr-fT_xUqsWPI-upg2w8Tli2bw/viewform).
- **Host a meetup:** have space for around 100 people interested in application security? [Offer a venue](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1XfF0xObIVGVUmQUCrwBj8WCnKO_kFpdy3HzzbyWEMB4).
- **Volunteer or support the chapter:** contact [Ori Troyna](mailto:ori.troyna@owasp.org).
- **Spread the word:** invite colleagues and people who are new to application security.

</div>
