Location and Time

The 2013 annual OWASP AppSec Israel conference was held at the Interdisciplinary Center in Herzliya (IDC) on October 1st, in the Efi Arazi school of Computer Science. With well over 450 participants, it was a huge and impressive success! You can find pictures of the event on ComSec facebook page

Thanks for participation in the event. Use the presentations info page to download presentations. Videos of most of the sessions can be watched here, courtesy of Checkmarx.
