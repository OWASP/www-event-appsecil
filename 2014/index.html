The annual OWASP AppSec Israel Conference is the largest conference in Israel for application security, and regularly draws over hundreds of participants. Around 450 attended this year!

AppSec Israel included 2 lecture tracks, and a sponsors display area for vendors of products and services relevant to Application Security.

Attending the conference was free of charge, as always.
