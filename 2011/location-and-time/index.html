Location and Time

The 2011 annual OWASP Israel conference was held at the Interdisciplinary Center Herzliya (IDC) on September 15th in the Efi Arazi school of computer science.
The conference in numbers: Over 350 attendees, 16 sponsors, 12 booths, and 14 excellent lectures.
